# Executive Brief

## Problem
Enterprise IT teams spend significant time gathering repetitive incident evidence and applying known remediation procedures. Pure chatbot approaches accelerate conversation but do not solve the operational-control problem.

## Proposal
Use an AI operations agent to perform bounded investigation, retrieve approved knowledge, recommend remediation and execute only explicitly authorized low-risk actions.

## Business value
- Faster incident resolution
- Lower repetitive support workload
- Consistent use of approved runbooks
- 24x7 first response
- Better operational auditability

## Control model
The AI model cannot widen permissions, access secrets, approve itself or execute privileged actions. Deterministic application policy remains the enforcement layer.
